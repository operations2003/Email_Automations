# TaskNera Domain Warmup Plan

## Current Status
- ✅ SPF, DKIM, DMARC properly configured
- ❌ New domain lacks sending reputation
- 🎯 Goal: Build reputation to reach primary inbox

## Week 1: Foundation (5 emails/day)
**Day 1-2:** Send to your own team emails
- operations@tasknera.com → your personal Gmail/Outlook
- Have team members reply and mark as "Important"
- Send 3-5 emails total

**Day 3-7:** Send to known contacts
- Friends, family, business contacts who know TaskNera
- Ask them to reply and move to primary folder
- Gradually increase to 5 emails/day

## Week 2: Controlled Expansion (10-15 emails/day)
**Targets:** 
- Previous customers/contacts
- People who have shown interest in TaskNera
- Warm leads who expect communication

**Actions:**
- Send personalized emails (not automated)
- Include "Reply if you're interested" CTAs
- Track open and reply rates

## Week 3: Early Automation (20-25 emails/day)
**Start using your system:**
- Target highly engaged prospects
- Companies that have visited your website
- Use your most natural, conversational templates
- Monitor spam complaints closely

## Week 4: Scale Up (30-40 emails/day)
**Full automation:**
- Use all outreach intents
- Monitor deliverability daily
- Adjust volume based on bounce rates

## Critical Success Metrics
- **Bounce Rate:** Keep under 3%
- **Spam Complaints:** Keep under 0.1% (1 in 1000)
- **Reply Rate:** Aim for 2-5%
- **Open Rate:** Aim for 20%+ (though Gmail limits tracking)

## Emergency Actions if Spam Issues Persist:
1. **Switch to individual Gmail accounts** temporarily
2. **Use different sending domains** (e.g., outreach.tasknera.com)
3. **Consider professional email services** (SendGrid, Mailgun, Postmark)
4. **Manual sending** for the first 100 emails to build reputation

## Tools to Monitor:
- Google Postmaster Tools: https://postmaster.google.com
- MXToolbox monitoring: https://mxtoolbox.com
- Gmail deliverability tests with personal accounts

## Red Flags to Watch:
- Emails bouncing back
- "Your domain has a bad reputation" messages
- Automatic spam folder placement even to known contacts
